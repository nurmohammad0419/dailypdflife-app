An offer letter, a tenancy contract or a school form arrives on WhatsApp with the message “please sign and send back today”. Printing it, signing with a pen and scanning it again takes time, and you may not even have a printer. You can do the whole job on your phone in a few minutes, and the result looks cleaner than a photographed page.

Step 1: Open the PDF in the editor

Save the PDF to your phone, then open the PDF editor and choose the file. The pages appear on screen. If the document is password-protected, unlock it first with Unlock PDF.

Step 2: Fill in the blanks

If the PDF has proper fillable boxes, Fill PDF forms is the fastest way to type your answers; save it, then come back to the editor to sign. If the form is just printed lines, choose the Text tool in the editor, tap on the line and type your name, the date, your ID or Iqama number and any other details. Make the text size match the form so it looks neat.

Step 3: Draw your signature

  1. Tap Signature.
  2. Turn your phone sideways for more room and sign with your finger, slowly, the way you sign on paper. A stylus gives an even better result.
  3. Tap Clear to try again, or Use it when you're happy.
  4. Drag the signature onto the signature line and resize it so it fits naturally, not too big.

Choose dark blue or black ink. Many offices prefer blue because it shows the document was signed rather than photocopied.

Step 4: Add the date, initials and seal

Contracts often need your initials at the bottom of every page. Add a small text box with your initials on each page, or place a small copy of your signature. Businesses can add the company seal with Stamp PDF: upload a photo of the seal on white paper and turn on “make the white background see-through” so it looks like a real stamp.

Step 5: Save and send

Tap Save PDF, open the saved file once to check every page, then send it back by email or WhatsApp. If the file is large, use Compress PDF first.

Is a drawn signature legally valid?

For everyday documents such as offer letters, school forms, quotations and many private contracts, a signature drawn on a PDF is widely accepted, in the same way as a scanned signed copy. Some government transactions, banking documents and high-value contracts require a certified digital signature or an official e-signature service, such as those offered through national platforms. When in doubt, ask the person who sent the document which form of signature they accept.

Keep your signature safe

Don't send a clean image of your signature to people you don't know, and don't leave it in shared folders. DailyPdfLife's editor runs in your browser, so your signature and documents are not uploaded to any server.
