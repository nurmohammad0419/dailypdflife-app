About Protect PDF

Protect PDF locks a document with a password so that nobody can open it without that password. It uses AES-256 encryption, the same strong standard used by Adobe Acrobat, and keeps your text, links and quality exactly as they were. You can also choose whether people who open the file may print it, copy its text or edit it.

How to password-protect a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Type a password. Tick Show password to check it is typed correctly.
  3. Choose what is allowed after opening: printing, copying text, editing.
  4. Tap Protect it and save the locked file.

Choosing a good password

Use at least 8 to 12 characters and mix words with numbers, for example a short phrase that only you and the receiver know. Do not send the password in the same email or chat message as the file; send it by SMS or say it on a call. Keep a copy of the password somewhere safe. Because the encryption is strong and done on your device, nobody, including DailyPdfLife, can open the file or recover the password for you.

When to protect a PDF

Protect salary slips, bank statements, contracts, medical reports, ID copies and customer invoices before sending them by email or storing them in shared folders. Many banks and companies already send statements this way; now you can do the same for your own documents.

Frequently asked questions

How strong is the protection?

The file is encrypted with AES-256, which cannot be broken by guessing in any practical amount of time if the password is good.

I forgot the password. Can you unlock it?

No. The password never leaves your device and there is no back door, so keep it safe.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes, open the file with its password in the Unlock PDF tool and save an unlocked copy.

Is my document uploaded?

No. The encryption happens in your browser.
