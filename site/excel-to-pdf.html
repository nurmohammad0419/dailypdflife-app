About Excel to PDF

Spreadsheets often print badly: columns spill onto extra pages and gridlines disappear. Excel to PDF lays your sheet out neatly on A4 or Letter pages, fits it to the page width, and lets you choose portrait or landscape, gridlines and a bold header row. It works with .xlsx, .xls, .csv and .ods files, and Arabic and Bangla text appear correctly.

How to convert Excel to PDF

  1. Choose your spreadsheet.
  2. Pick which sheet to include, or all sheets.
  3. Choose page size and orientation. Landscape is best for wide tables.
  4. Keep Fit to page width on so no columns are cut off. Turn gridlines and a bold first row on or off.
  5. Tap Save as PDF, choose Save as PDF in the print window and tick Background graphics.

Where it helps

Send price lists, timesheets, attendance sheets, material lists, quotations and reports to clients who don't have Excel, or attach them to emails in a format that can't be changed by accident. To go the other way, PDF to Excel pulls tables from a PDF back into a spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

Which file types are supported?

.xlsx, .xls, .csv and .ods.

My table is too wide. What should I do?

Choose Landscape and keep Fit to page width turned on.

Are formulas kept?

The PDF shows the calculated values, as a printed sheet would.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded?

No, it's converted on your own device.
