About the End of service calculator

When a job ends in Saudi Arabia or the UAE, the employer usually owes an end-of-service benefit, also called gratuity or EOSB. The amount depends on how long you worked, your wage and, in Saudi Arabia, how the job ended. Enter your dates and wage to see the amount and how it was worked out.

How it is calculated

  • Saudi Arabia (Labour Law Articles 84 and 85): half a month's wage for each of the first five years and one month's wage for each year after, based on the last wage, with part-years counted. If you resign, you get nothing under 2 years, one third for 2 to 5 years, two thirds for 5 to 10 years and the full amount after 10 years.
  • UAE (Decree-Law 33 of 2021, Article 51): 21 days of basic wage per year for the first five years and 30 days per year after, for at least one year of full-time service, up to a maximum of two years' wage. Under the current law, resigning does not reduce it.

Important

This calculator gives an estimate for typical cases. Special situations change the result: dismissal under Saudi Article 80, leaving under Article 81, women resigning within set periods after marriage or childbirth, part-time contracts, unpaid absences and amounts the employer may legally deduct. Check your contract and confirm with the Ministry of Human Resources (HRSD) in Saudi Arabia or MOHRE in the UAE before you rely on the figure.

Frequently asked questions

Which wage is used in Saudi Arabia?

The last actual wage, which normally includes basic salary and fixed allowances such as housing.

Which wage is used in the UAE?

The basic salary only.

Do I get gratuity if I resign in Saudi Arabia before 2 years?

Not under Article 85, unless a legal exception applies.

Is my data saved?

No, the calculation happens on your device.
