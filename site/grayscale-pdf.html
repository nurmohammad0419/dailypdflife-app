About Grayscale PDF

Printing colour pages on a colour printer uses expensive ink, and some offices and portals ask for black-and-white copies. Grayscale PDF turns every page into shades of grey, or into crisp black and white that makes scanned text easier to read. It also often makes scanned documents smaller.

How to make a PDF grayscale

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Pick Grayscale for photos and charts, or Black & white for text documents.
  3. Choose the quality: Normal for email, Print for paper.
  4. Tap Make it grayscale and save.

Good to know

Pages are redrawn as images, so text in the new file can't be selected or searched. If you need that, keep the colour original for searching and use the grayscale copy for printing. To reduce the size further, run the result through Compress PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Will this save printer ink?

Yes, printers use only black ink for grayscale pages when set to print in black and white.

Which option is best for scanned letters?

Black & white usually gives the cleanest, most readable result.

Is the text still selectable?

No, pages become images.

Is my file uploaded?

No.
