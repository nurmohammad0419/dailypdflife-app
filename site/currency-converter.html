About the Currency converter

Whether you're sending money home, paying a supplier abroad or comparing prices, this converter shows how much your money is worth in another currency. It includes the Gulf currencies, taka, Indian and Pakistani rupee, and the world's main currencies, and shows a table of your currency against all the others. Rates update daily and the last update time is shown.

How to convert

  1. Type the amount.
  2. Choose the currency you have and the one you want. Tap ⇄ to swap them.
  3. Read the result and the rate in both directions.

Rates you see vs. rates you get

The rate shown is the mid-market rate, halfway between buying and selling prices. Banks, exchange houses and money transfer apps add a margin and sometimes a fee, so the amount your family receives will be slightly lower. Compare the final amount, not only the advertised rate, before you send. Your last choice of currencies is remembered on this device.

Frequently asked questions

How often are rates updated?

Once a day, from Exchange Rate API.

Does it work offline?

It uses the last rates saved on your device, and you can type a rate yourself.

Why is the bank's rate different?

Banks add a margin to the mid-market rate.

Which currencies are included?

More than 20, including SAR, BDT, AED, USD, EUR, GBP, INR and PKR.
