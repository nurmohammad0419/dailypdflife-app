About PDF to PNG

PNG keeps text and lines perfectly sharp, which makes it the best image format for documents, diagrams, certificates and slides. This tool turns the pages you choose into PNG images at screen, high or 300 dpi print resolution. It can also make WEBP images for websites or JPG for smaller files, and PNG pages can have a transparent background.

How to convert PDF to PNG

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Pick the format and resolution. 144 dpi is ideal for screens; 300 dpi for printing.
  3. Type page numbers such as 1-3, 5, or leave empty for all pages.
  4. Tap Convert to images and save the pictures.

PNG, JPG or WEBP?

Choose PNG for documents and anything with text; it never adds blurry edges. JPG files are smaller and fine for photos. WEBP gives the smallest files with good quality and works in all modern browsers. A transparent background is useful for logos and signatures exported from a PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What resolution should I choose?

High (144 dpi) for screens and sharing, Print (300 dpi) for paper.

Can I convert only one page?

Yes, type its number in the Pages box.

Why are 300 dpi PNG files large?

PNG is lossless. Choose WEBP or JPG for smaller files.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No.
