About the Signature maker

A clean digital signature is handy for signing PDFs, adding to Word letters and invoices, and uploading to job or exam forms. Draw your signature with your finger or mouse, type your name in one of five handwriting styles, or photograph your real signature on paper and let the tool remove the background. Choose the ink colour and download a transparent PNG or a JPG on white.

How to make your signature

  1. Choose Draw, Type or From photo.
  2. Draw slowly, or type your name and pick a style, or upload your photo and adjust the clean-up strength.
  3. Pick black, blue, green or dark red ink.
  4. Download the PNG (transparent) or JPG.

Using your signature

The PNG has no background, so it sits naturally on any document. Open a PDF in Sign PDF and your new signature is already loaded. In Word, insert it as a picture and set wrapping to “In front of text”. If a form needs a signature under 20 KB, use Resize image to KB. Keep your signature file private.

Frequently asked questions

What is a transparent PNG?

An image without a background, so only the ink shows on top of your document.

Is a typed signature valid?

For many everyday documents yes, but a drawn or photographed signature looks more personal.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, draw with your finger.

Is my signature uploaded?

No, it stays on your device.
