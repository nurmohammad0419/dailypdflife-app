About PDF to text

Sometimes you just need the words: to paste a paragraph into an email, to translate a document, to search it, or to feed it into another program. PDF to text pulls every word out of a PDF, page by page, into plain text you can copy or save as a .txt file. Bangla, Arabic and English text all work when the PDF contains real text.

How to extract text from a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Keep Mark where each page starts on if you want page numbers in the text.
  3. Turn on Join broken lines to turn short PDF lines back into flowing paragraphs.
  4. Tap Extract text, then copy it or save the .txt file.

If you get no text

A scanned PDF or a photo of a page contains a picture of the words, not the words themselves, so there is nothing to extract. Use OCR instead, which reads the text from the picture. If copied Bangla text looks broken, read our guide on why Bangla PDF text breaks.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep formatting?

No, it produces plain text. Use PDF to Word to keep paragraphs in a document.

Can it read scanned PDFs?

No, use OCR for those.

Which encoding is the .txt file?

UTF-8, which opens correctly in Notepad, Word and phones.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No.
