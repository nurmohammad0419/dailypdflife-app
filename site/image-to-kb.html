About Resize image to KB

Job portals, visa and Iqama services, university admissions and exam forms often say “photo must be under 50 KB” or “signature under 20 KB”. This tool finds the best quality that fits the size you choose, and makes the picture smaller only if it has to. If the form also asks for exact pixels, such as 300 × 300, type them in and the photo is cropped neatly to fit.

How to reduce a photo to a size in KB

  1. Choose your photo.
  2. Tap a target size such as 50 KB, or type your own.
  3. Fill in width and height only if the form asks for exact pixels.
  4. Tap Make it this size and compare before and after.
  5. Save the photo and upload it.

Tips

For passport-style photos, make the photo first in the Passport photo maker and then reduce it here. For a signature under 20 KB, create it in the Signature maker and save as JPG. iPhone HEIC photos must be converted with HEIC to JPG first.

Frequently asked questions

Will the photo look blurry?

The tool keeps the highest quality that fits. Very small targets on large photos may soften details.

How do I make a 300×300 photo under 50 KB?

Type 300 in width and height and choose 50 KB.

Which format do forms accept?

Most want JPG, which is the default.

Is my photo uploaded?
