Zakat is one of the five pillars of Islam: 2.5% of qualifying wealth, paid once a lunar year by every Muslim whose wealth stays above a minimum amount called the nisab. The idea is simple, but questions come up every year: Which gold counts? How do I value 21-karat jewellery? Do I subtract my loan? This guide walks through the usual method with an example. The Zakat calculator does the arithmetic for you.

Step 1: Choose your zakat date

Zakat is due when your wealth has stayed at or above the nisab for one full lunar (Hijri) year, called the hawl. Many people pick a fixed date, such as 1 Ramadan, and calculate every year on that day. Use the Hijri date converter to find the matching Gregorian date each year.

Step 2: Work out the nisab

The nisab is the value of 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver on your zakat date. Check today's price per gram with a jeweller or bank.

  • If 24K gold is SAR 400 per gram: gold nisab = 85 × 400 = SAR 34,000.
  • If silver is SAR 5 per gram: silver nisab = 595 × 5 = SAR 2,975.

Because silver is much cheaper, the silver nisab is far lower, so more people pass it. Many scholars recommend the silver nisab as the more cautious choice for cash savings; others use gold. Follow the view of a scholar you trust.

Step 3: Add up what you own

  • Cash at home and in your wallet.
  • Money in current and savings accounts.
  • Gold and silver, valued at today's price.
  • Money others owe you that you expect to receive.
  • Shares, funds and investments at today's value.
  • Goods you hold for sale in a business, at selling price.

Your home, car, furniture, clothes and the tools you use for work do not count.

Valuing 21K and 22K gold

Jewellery in the Gulf and South Asia is usually 21K or 22K, which is not pure gold. Multiply the weight by the karat and divide by 24 to get the pure gold content: 50 grams of 21K gold = 50 × 21 ÷ 24 = 43.75 grams of pure gold. Multiply by the 24K price to get its value. For weights in tola, 1 tola = 11.664 grams; the Unit converter converts for you. Scholars differ on whether gold jewellery that is worn regularly is zakatable; many say it is, others exempt it.

Step 4: Subtract debts due now

Subtract debts and bills you must pay soon, such as an instalment due this month, unpaid rent or salaries you owe. For long-term loans such as home finance, many scholars allow subtracting only the payments currently due, not the whole balance.

Worked example

ItemValue (SAR)
Bank savings28,000
Cash at home2,000
50 g of 21K gold (43.75 g pure × 400)17,500
Money a friend owes you3,000
Minus: credit card bill due now−2,500
Net zakatable wealth48,000

48,000 is above the nisab whether you use gold (34,000) or silver (2,975), so zakat is due: 48,000 × 2.5% = SAR 1,200. The prices in this example are only for illustration.

Who receives zakat

The Quran (9:60) names eight groups, including the poor, the needy and those in debt. Many people give through trusted charities or official platforms, or directly to relatives in need who are eligible. This guide explains a general method; for your own situation, especially business assets, pensions or shared property, ask a qualified scholar.
