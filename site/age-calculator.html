Age

About the Age calculator

The age calculator gives your exact age in years, months and days. Enter a date of birth in the Gregorian or Hijri calendar and see the age today, or on any other date you choose, which is useful when a form asks for your age on a specific deadline. It also shows totals such as weeks and days lived and the countdown to your next birthday.

How to calculate age

  1. Choose Gregorian or Hijri and enter the date of birth.
  2. Leave Age on this date as today, or pick another date.
  3. Read the exact age in years, months and days.

When you need it

Visa, Iqama, school admission, job and exam applications often have age limits counted on a specific date, for example “must be under 35 on 1 January”. Insurance and retirement rules also depend on exact age. Because Hijri years are about 11 days shorter than Gregorian years, a person's Hijri age can be one year more than their Gregorian age. To convert a single date between calendars, use the Hijri date converter.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter a Hijri date of birth?

Yes, switch to Hijri and enter it; the Umm al-Qura calendar is used.

Why is my Hijri age different?

Hijri years are about 354 days long, so you have lived more Hijri years than Gregorian years.

Can I find my age on a future date?

Yes, change Age on this date to any date.

Is my date of birth saved?

No, the calculation happens in your browser.
