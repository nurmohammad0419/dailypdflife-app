About Prayer times

See today's prayer times for your city, a live countdown to the next prayer, and a table for the next seven days. Pick from cities across Saudi Arabia, the Gulf, Bangladesh, Pakistan, India and beyond, or use your phone's location. Times are calculated on your device from the sun's position, so they work even offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose your city, or tap Use my location.
  2. The calculation method is set for your country, for example Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia or Karachi in Bangladesh. You can change it.
  3. Choose Hanafi for a later Asr time if you follow that school.
  4. Pick another date to see times for any day.

About the methods

Methods differ mainly in the sun angle used for Fajr and Isha. Umm al-Qura, used in Saudi Arabia, sets Isha 90 minutes after Maghrib; in Ramadan many mosques there use 120 minutes. Bangladesh and Pakistan commonly use the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method. Your local mosque's timetable may differ by a few minutes; follow it where you pray.

Frequently asked questions

Which method should I choose?

The one used by your local mosques; the default for your country is a good start.

What does the Hanafi option change?

Only Asr, which becomes later.

Does it need internet?

Only to load the page; times are calculated on your device.

Is my location sent anywhere?

No, it is used only on your device.
