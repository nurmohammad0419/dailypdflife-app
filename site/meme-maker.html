About the Meme maker

Turn any photo into a meme. Choose the classic look with big white letters and a black outline at the top and bottom, or the modern caption style with a white bar of text above the picture, as seen on social media. You can also start with a plain coloured background for text-only posts. There's no watermark and nothing is uploaded.

How to make a meme

  1. Choose a photo, or a plain background colour.
  2. Pick Classic or Caption bar on top.
  3. Type your text, and adjust the size and colour.
  4. Tap Save meme and share it.

Make it land

Short text works best: a few words at the top set up the joke and the bottom line delivers it. Use photos you took yourself or have permission to use, and keep jokes kind. For more text styles and several text boxes, try Text on photo; to combine pictures first, use Photo collage.
