      About Merge PDF and JPG to PDF

      This page has two everyday tools. Merge PDF joins several PDF files into a single document, in the order you choose. JPG to PDF turns one or more photos or screenshots into a PDF, with each image on its own page. Both run entirely in your browser, so they are quick even on a slow connection and your documents stay private.

      How to merge PDF files

      1. Under Merge PDF, choose the PDF files you want to join. On a computer, hold Ctrl (or Cmd on Mac) to pick several at once.
      2. The files are joined in the order you pick them, so select the first document first.
      3. Tap Merge PDF and save the combined file.

      Need to change the page order afterwards or drop a page? Open the result in Organize PDF.

      How to turn images into a PDF

      1. Under JPG to PDF, choose one or more JPG or PNG images.
      2. Tap Convert to PDF. Every image becomes one page.
      3. Save the PDF and share it by email or WhatsApp.

      Common uses

      Merging is ideal when an office or embassy asks for “one PDF file”: put your application form, passport copy, Iqama or ID copy and supporting letters into a single document. JPG to PDF is the quickest way to send photographed receipts, certificates or handwritten notes as a proper document. If your photos are of paper pages, Scan to PDF also straightens and cleans them so they look like a real scan.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is there a limit on the number of files?

      There is no fixed limit. Very large files depend on the memory of your device; most phones handle dozens of normal documents easily.

      Will merging reduce the quality?

      No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays sharp and selectable.

      Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

      Remove the password first with the Unlock PDF tool (you need to know the password), then merge.

      The merged file is too big to email. What can I do?

      Run it through Compress PDF to make it smaller before sending.
