About Unlock PDF

Banks, telecom companies and employers often send statements and salary slips as password-protected PDFs. Typing the password every time is tiresome, and some upload portals refuse locked files. Unlock PDF removes the password, and any printing or copying restrictions, from a PDF you are allowed to open. The text, pages and quality stay exactly the same.

How to unlock a PDF

  1. Choose the locked PDF.
  2. Type its password. Tap Show to check it.
  3. Tap Unlock PDF and save the unlocked copy.

Finding the password

Banks usually explain the password in the email or SMS that came with the statement. It is often a combination such as part of your name with your date of birth, or the last digits of your account, ID or mobile number. Check that message carefully. This tool does not guess or crack passwords: you need to know the password, and you should only unlock files you own or have permission to change.

Frequently asked questions

Can it unlock a PDF without the password?

No. If a PDF needs a password to open, you must enter it. Only restrictions on printing and copying can be removed from files that open without a password.

Is the unlocked file the same quality?

Yes. Nothing is converted or redrawn; only the encryption is removed.

Is it safe to unlock bank statements here?

Yes. The file and password stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Can I add a new password afterwards?

Yes, use Protect PDF to lock it again with a password of your choice.
