About the Passport Photo Maker

Make your own passport, visa, Iqama, residency or ID card photos without visiting a studio. Choose the official size and the tool finds your face and places it at the right head size automatically, straightening a tilted head too. Fine-tune with zoom, pinch or drag, check the live head-size and centring checks, change the background to white or another colour with AI, and download a single photo for online applications or a full sheet ready to print at any photo shop.

How to make a passport photo

  1. Choose a clear, front-facing photo taken in good light.
  2. Pick the size: 35 × 45 mm (most passports and visas), 40 × 60 mm, 45 × 55 mm, 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm, USA), 30 × 40 mm, or a custom size.
  3. Drag and zoom so your face sits inside the red oval. Adjust brightness and contrast if needed.
  4. Tap Remove background and pick white or the colour your country asks for.
  5. Download a single photo, a 4×6 print sheet or an A4 print sheet.

Taking a good photo

Stand facing the camera about one metre from a plain wall, with light in front of you rather than behind. Keep a neutral expression with eyes open and mouth closed, don't wear glasses if the rules forbid them, and keep your hair away from your eyes. Ask someone else to take the photo at eye level instead of using a selfie, which distorts the face. Rules differ by country and by embassy, so always check the official requirements before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a standard passport photo?

Most countries, including Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bangladesh, the UK and the EU, use 35 × 45 mm. The USA uses 2 × 2 inches.

Can I print the sheet at a shop?

Yes, download the 4×6 sheet and print it as a normal 4×6 inch photo; you get several copies at once.

Is my photo uploaded?

No, the background removal and editing run on your device.

What background colour do I need?

White is most common; some visas ask for light blue or off-white. Check the official rules.
