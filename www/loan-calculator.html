About the Loan & EMI calculator

Before you sign for a car loan, personal finance or home finance, it helps to know exactly what you will pay. Enter the amount, the yearly rate and the period, and this calculator shows the monthly payment (EMI), the total profit or interest, and the total you will pay. It supports both flat-rate and reducing-balance methods, which banks in the Gulf and South Asia commonly use, and works in SAR, AED, BDT, USD and more.

How to use it

  1. Enter the loan amount and choose your currency.
  2. Enter the yearly profit or interest rate.
  3. Enter the time to pay back in years or months.
  4. Choose Flat rate or Reducing balance, as stated in the bank's offer.
  5. Add a down payment if you have one, then read the results and the payment plan by year or month.

Flat rate vs reducing balance

With a flat rate, profit is charged on the full original amount for the whole period, even though you pay it down every month. With a reducing balance, profit is charged only on what you still owe. That means a 4% flat rate costs far more than a 4% reducing rate: in practice almost twice as much. Many car and personal finance offers quote a flat rate, so always check which method is used, and ask for the APR to compare offers fairly. Banks may add fees or insurance, so the final offer can differ.

Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?

EMI means equated monthly instalment: the fixed amount you pay each month.

Which is cheaper, flat or reducing?

At the same rate, reducing balance is always cheaper.

Does it include bank fees?

No, add admin fees and insurance separately to the total.

Is my data saved?

No, calculations happen in your browser.
