About HEIC to JPG

iPhones save photos in the HEIC format to save space. Many websites, Windows computers, older Android phones and government or job portals cannot open HEIC files. This tool converts them to the universal JPG format, or to PNG, right in your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose your .heic or .heif photos, or drag them onto the page. You can pick many at once.
  2. Choose JPG or PNG and set the quality. 90% gives a sharp picture with a sensible file size.
  3. Tap Convert, then Download all.

Stop your iPhone saving HEIC

If you often need JPG, you can change the camera setting on your iPhone: go to Settings → Camera → Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos will then be saved as JPG. Photos you already took stay HEIC, and this converter handles those.

Frequently asked questions

Will I lose quality converting HEIC to JPG?

At 90% or higher the difference is not visible for normal use.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes, select as many as you like and download them all together.

Should I choose JPG or PNG?

JPG is smaller and works everywhere. PNG is larger but lossless.

Are my photos uploaded?

No, the conversion happens on your device.
