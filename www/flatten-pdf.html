About Flatten PDF

A filled PDF form can still be edited by anyone who opens it, and some programs show the answers differently or even lose them. Flattening turns the answers, ticks and dropdown choices into normal page content, so the document looks the same everywhere and can no longer be changed. Choose Lock form fields to keep text sharp and searchable, or Flatten everything to turn each page into a single picture, including comments, highlights and stamps.

How to flatten a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Pick Lock form fields or Flatten everything.
  3. For “Flatten everything”, choose the quality.
  4. Tap Flatten PDF and save the new file.

When to flatten

Flatten the final copy of an application form before you send it, invoices and quotations you don't want changed, and documents with comments or stamps that must print correctly. Keep your editable original in case you need to change something later. To fill the form first, use Fill PDF forms.

Frequently asked questions

Will the text stay searchable?

With Lock form fields, yes. Flatten everything turns pages into pictures, so text can't be selected.

Can I undo flattening?

No, so keep your original file.

Why does my PDF say it has no form fields?

It may be a printed form without fillable boxes. Use Flatten everything instead.

Is my file uploaded?

No, flattening happens in your browser.
