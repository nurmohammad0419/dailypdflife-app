Visa applications, residency renewals, family visit requests, university admissions and job portals all ask for the same kind of thing: clear copies of your documents, uploaded as PDF or JPG, under a certain size. Most rejections and delays come from small problems such as a blurry passport copy, a file that's too big, or pages uploaded in the wrong order. This checklist helps you get everything right the first time.

1. Read the requirements first

Before scanning anything, note exactly what the portal or office asks for:

  • Which documents, and whether each one should be a separate file or all in one.
  • Allowed formats (PDF, JPG, PNG) and the maximum size per file, often 1 MB or 2 MB, sometimes as low as 200 KB.
  • Whether colour copies are required, and whether translations or attestations are needed.
  • Photo rules: size, background colour, file size in KB and sometimes exact pixels.

Write these down; it saves re-doing the work later.

2. Scan each document properly

Use Scan to PDF on your phone. Put the document on a dark surface in good daylight, let the tool find the edges, and choose:

  • Enhanced colour for passports, ID cards, Iqama, driving licences and anything with photos, stamps or holograms.
  • Document (black and white) for typed letters, contracts and statements, which gives smaller, sharper files.

For a passport, scan the photo page and, if asked, the page with your current visa or entry stamp. For ID cards, scan the front and back.

3. Get your photo right

Use the Passport photo maker to set the correct size and background. If the portal gives a KB limit, use Compress image; if it gives pixels, use Resize image first. Our passport photo size guide lists common sizes.

4. Combine, order and shrink

  1. If the portal wants one file, use Merge PDF and pick the files in the order they are listed in the requirements.
  2. Fix any page that is sideways or out of order with Organize PDF.
  3. Check the size. If it's over the limit, use Compress PDF on Medium, and zoom in afterwards to make sure numbers and names are still readable.
  4. Remove password protection from bank statements with Unlock PDF, as most portals can't open locked files.

5. Name files clearly

Use names that say what each file is, for example Passport-Rahman-Ahmed.pdf, Salary-Certificate-2026-11.pdf or Bank-Statement-Aug-Oct-2026.pdf. Avoid names like IMG_20261127_0932.pdf; officers and recruiters notice the difference, and you'll find the right file faster next time.

6. Check before you submit

  • Open every file once on your phone: are all pages there, straight and readable?
  • Are expiry dates valid? Many applications need a passport valid for at least six months.
  • Do names match exactly across documents, including spelling and order?

7. Keep a safe set for next time

Save your final, clean copies in one private folder on your phone or computer so the next renewal takes minutes instead of hours. When you send copies of IDs to agents or landlords, you can mark them with their purpose using Image watermark, for example “Copy for visa application only”, which makes misuse harder.
