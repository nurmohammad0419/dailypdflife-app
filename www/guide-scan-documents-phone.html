You don't need a scanner to send a professional-looking copy of a contract, a certificate or a signed form. A modern phone camera is sharp enough, as long as you take the photo well and clean it up afterwards. Sending a plain photo taken at an angle on a kitchen table looks careless and is often hard to read; a proper scan looks like it came from an office machine.

Before you start

  • Light: daylight near a window is best. Avoid the flash, which creates a bright spot, and make sure your own shadow is not over the page.
  • Background: put white paper on a dark table so the edges are easy to detect.
  • Flatness: unfold the page and hold the corners down if it curls.
  • Clean lens: wipe the camera lens; fingerprints make photos hazy.

Scanning step by step

  1. Open Scan to PDF and tap Take a photo.
  2. Hold the phone straight above the page so the page fills most of the frame.
  3. Tap Find page edges. Check the corners and drag any that are wrong.
  4. Choose a look. Document gives clean black and white, best for printed text. Enhanced colour keeps stamps, logos and blue-ink signatures. Grayscale is a middle ground.
  5. Repeat for each page, in order.
  6. Pick A4 page size and tap Make PDF.

On Android, the DailyPdfLife app also includes Google's document scanner, which detects the page and takes the photo automatically.

After scanning

Fix the order or rotation in Organize PDF if a page ended up in the wrong place or sideways.

Make the text searchable or editable with OCR, which reads English, Bangla, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu. This is useful for long documents you need to search or for retyping a letter.

Sign it by opening the PDF in the PDF editor and drawing your signature, so you don't need to print, sign and scan again.

Reduce the size with Compress PDF if it's too large for email or a portal.

Scanning ID cards and passports

For an ID card, scan the front and back as two pages of the same PDF, or put both photos on one page with Photo collage using the A4 layout. Use Enhanced colour so the holograms and photo stay clear. Before sending a copy of an ID, consider writing its purpose across it with Image watermark, for example “Copy for tenancy contract only”, so it can't easily be misused.

Privacy

Many scanner apps upload your documents to the cloud. DailyPdfLife's Scan to PDF works inside your browser, so your contracts, IDs and certificates stay on your phone until you choose to send them.
