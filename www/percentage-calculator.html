The formulas

X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. Percent of = part ÷ whole × 100. Change % = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Price after discount = price × (1 − discount ÷ 100). Original value = part ÷ percent × 100. The calculator does these for you, but knowing them helps you check a shop's offer or a salary letter.

Everyday examples

Work out exam marks as a percentage, check whether a “50% off then extra 20% off” sale is really 70% (it's actually 60%), see how much your rent or salary changed, or add a markup to your cost price. For tax, the VAT calculator adds or removes VAT in one step.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

Is 50% off plus 20% off the same as 70% off?

No, it's 60% off, because the second discount applies to the already reduced price.

How do I remove a percentage from a total?

Use Find the original, or the VAT calculator for tax.
