About DailyPdfLife

DailyPdfLife is a free collection of everyday PDF, image and document tools — built for people who just need to get a file done, quickly and privately.

Why we built it

Most online PDF services ask you to upload your documents to their servers. For contracts, invoices, ID copies or personal papers, that is not always comfortable. DailyPdfLife takes a different approach: every tool runs inside your own browser, so your files never leave your device.

What you can do

PDF toolsMerge, split, compress, edit, organize, crop, rotate, redact, stamp, fill forms, number pages, watermark, remove or extract pages, lock and unlock PDFs.
ConvertersPDF to Word, Word to PDF, PDF to Excel, Excel to PDF, PDF to JPG, JPG to PDF, HEIC to JPG and Scan to PDF with your camera.
Image toolsRemove backgrounds, crop, compress, resize and convert images, make collages and passport photos, add text and watermarks, and blur private details.
Document makersBuild a CV, invoices and quotations, and cover letters, then save them as PDF.
Smart toolsOCR in English, Bangla, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu, QR codes, a Hijri date converter, and calculators for VAT, zakat, loans, percentages, age, units and building materials.
Two languagesThe whole site works in English and Bangla.

Our principles

  • Private by design — no uploads, no accounts, no file storage.
  • Free — the tools are free to use; advertising helps keep them that way.
  • Simple — open a tool, add your file, download the result.
