      About ZIP files

      A ZIP file packs many files into one, which makes them easy to attach to an email or upload to a portal that accepts only one file. Phones often can't open ZIP files someone sends you. This page does both: make a ZIP from any files, and open a ZIP to see and save what's inside.

      How to make or open a ZIP

      1. To make one: choose your files (you can add more in several goes), type a name and tap Create ZIP.
      2. To open one: choose the .zip file. The files inside are listed; save one with ⬇ or tap Save all files.

      Good to know

      ZIP compression shrinks text files, spreadsheets and Word documents a lot, but photos, videos and PDFs are already compressed and barely get smaller; use Compress PDF or Compress image for those first. Password-protected ZIP files can't be opened here.

      Frequently asked questions

      Can I open RAR or 7z files?

      No, only ZIP files.

      Does it work on iPhone and Android?

      Yes, in Safari and Chrome.

      Is there a size limit?

      It depends on your device's memory; a few hundred MB is usually fine.

      Are my files uploaded?

      No.
