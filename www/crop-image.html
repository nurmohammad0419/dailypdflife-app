About Crop image

Crop image cuts a photo down to just the part you want. Use free shape cropping, or lock it to a common ratio such as 1:1 for profile pictures and Instagram, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails and presentations, or 9:16 for WhatsApp status and stories. You can also rotate the photo left or right and flip it like a mirror.

How to crop an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  2. Pick a shape: Any shape, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:4 or 9:16.
  3. Drag the crop box and its corners over the area you want to keep.
  4. Use Left, Right or Flip if needed.
  5. Choose JPG, PNG or WEBP and tap Crop & save.

Useful crops

Cut a document out of a photo before sending it, remove a stranger from the edge of a picture, crop a screenshot to just the important message, or prepare a square photo for a CV or profile. To hide private information inside the picture instead of cutting it away, use Blur & hide.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping lower the quality?

No, the part you keep stays at its original sharpness.

What ratio should I use for Instagram?

1:1 for square posts, 4:5 or 3:4 for portrait posts, and 9:16 for stories.

Can I rotate without cropping?

Yes, rotate or flip and keep the crop box over the whole picture.

Is my photo uploaded?

No, everything is done on your device.
