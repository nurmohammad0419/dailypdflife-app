When you call

  1. Stay calm and say what happened: fire, accident, someone is unconscious, a crime.
  2. Give the exact location: city, district, street, a landmark, or share your live location.
  3. Say how many people are hurt and if anyone is not breathing or bleeding heavily.
  4. Don't hang up until the operator tells you to. Keep your phone free for their call back.
  5. In most countries you can call the emergency number even with no credit or no SIM card.

About Emergency numbers

In an emergency there is no time to search. Choose your country and city to see the main emergency number in large print and every other important line: police, ambulance, fire, traffic accidents, health advice and helplines for women and children. Tap any number to call it. The page also finds the nearest hospital, police station, pharmacy or embassy on the map and can send your exact location to family on WhatsApp.

How to use it

  1. Choose your country and city. Your choice is remembered on this phone.
  2. Tap the big red number for the main emergency service, or any other line below it.
  3. Under Find the nearest, tap a place to open it on the map near you.
  4. Tap Send my location on WhatsApp to share your exact position.

Important for Saudi Arabia

The unified number 911 works in the Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah and Eastern Province regions. In other regions, call 999 for police, 997 for an ambulance and 998 for civil defence; the page shows the right numbers for the city you pick. For minor road accidents with no injuries, call Najm. Add this page to your home screen so it opens even without internet, and save the main numbers in your phone's contacts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I call without credit or a SIM card?

In most countries the emergency number works from any mobile, even without credit.

What is the emergency number in Bangladesh?

999 for police, fire service and ambulance; it is free.

Which number is for ambulances in the UAE?

998. Police is 999 and fire is 997.

Is my location shared with DailyPdfLife?

No, it is used only on your phone to open the map or WhatsApp.
