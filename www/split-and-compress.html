About Split PDF and Compress PDF

Split PDF breaks a document apart, either into one file per page or into the page ranges you type, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10. Compress PDF makes a file smaller so it fits email attachment limits and the upload size limits of government, visa and job portals. Both tools work in your browser, so your documents are never uploaded.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF under Split PDF.
  2. Pick Every page as its own file, or choose Specific pages and type the pages you want, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10.
  3. Tap Split PDF and save the new files.

How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF under Compress PDF.
  2. Pick a level: Light keeps the best quality, Medium is the best balance for most files, and Heavy makes the smallest file.
  3. Tap Compress PDF. The new size is shown so you can compare it with the original.

Tips for the smallest file

Compression works best on PDFs made from scans and photos, because the images inside can be stored more efficiently. A PDF that is mostly text is usually small already and will shrink less. If a portal asks for a file under 1 MB or 2 MB, start with Medium and only try Heavy if it is still too big. You can also remove pages you don't need with Remove pages before compressing.

Frequently asked questions

Does compressing a PDF reduce quality?

Light and Medium keep text sharp and photos clear for normal viewing. Heavy compression lowers image quality more to reach the smallest size.

Can I split out just one page?

Yes. Choose Specific pages and type the page number, for example 4.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Splitting and compressing happen on your own device.

Why did my file barely get smaller?

It probably contains mostly text, which is already compact. Scanned or photo-heavy PDFs shrink the most.
