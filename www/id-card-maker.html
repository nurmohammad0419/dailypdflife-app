About the ID card maker

Make professional ID cards without design software. Choose Employee, Student, Event or Visitor, pick one of four designs in portrait or landscape, add the organisation logo, the person's photo and details, and the back of the card with address, emergency contact, a QR code and an authorised signature. Cards are made at the standard 85.6 × 54 mm (CR80) size at 300 dpi, so they print sharp.

How to make an ID card

  1. Choose the card type, design, shape and colour.
  2. Type the organisation name and add the logo.
  3. Add the photo, name, role, ID number and other details.
  4. Fill in the back: address, emergency contact and a note. The QR code is made from the name and ID unless you type your own text or link.
  5. Download a card-size PDF, an A4 sheet with 8 cards for printing, or PNG images.

Printing tips

Print the A4 sheet at 100% (“Actual size”) on thick card paper, 250 to 300 gsm, then cut along the grey lines and laminate. For double-sided cards, print the fronts, put the sheet back in the printer and print the backs; the backs are mirrored so they line up. Many print shops can also print PVC cards from the PNG files. Use a clear, front-facing photo; the Passport photo tool can make one with a plain background.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a standard ID card?

85.6 × 54 mm, the same as a bank card (CR80).

Can I make cards for many people?

Yes: make one, download it, then change the name, photo and ID for the next. Your design is remembered.

Can I put a QR code on the card?

Yes, it's on the back. Type a link or text, or leave it empty to use the name and ID.

Is the information uploaded?

No, cards are made on your device.
