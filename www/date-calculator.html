About the Date calculator

How many days until a visa expires? What date is 90 days after the contract starts? How many working days are in this month? The Date calculator answers these in two modes: Days between dates gives the total days, years-months-days, weeks and working days; Add or subtract gives the date after or before a number of days, weeks, months or years.

How to use it

  1. Choose a mode.
  2. For days between: pick the start and end dates and your weekend, such as Friday + Saturday for Saudi Arabia or Saturday + Sunday for the UAE.
  3. Tick Count the end date too if both days should count, as with leave requests.
  4. For add or subtract: pick a date and the amount to add, and optionally skip weekends.

Useful for

Notice periods, probation end dates, visit visa and Iqama validity, payment due dates, pregnancy and exam countdowns, project deadlines and leave planning. Public holidays are not subtracted from working days, so remove them yourself if needed. To convert a date to Hijri, use the Hijri date converter.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count public holidays?

No, only the weekend days you choose are skipped.

Should I count the end date?

For leave and contract periods, usually yes. For “days until”, no.

How are months added to the 31st?

If the month is shorter, the last day of that month is used.

Is anything saved?

No.
