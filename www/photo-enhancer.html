About the Photo enhancer

Phone photos taken indoors or in the evening often come out dark, grey or slightly soft. The Photo enhancer fixes that: Auto enhance balances the light and colours and sharpens the details in one tap, and the sliders let you fine-tune brightness, contrast, colour, warmth, shadows, sharpness and vignette. Presets give quick looks for documents, vivid colours, warm tones or black and white.

How to enhance a photo

  1. Choose a photo.
  2. Tap Auto enhance, or a preset such as Document for photographed papers.
  3. Adjust the sliders if you like. Hold See original to compare.
  4. Choose JPG, PNG or WEBP and save. The full-resolution photo is processed.

Tips

The Document preset makes photographed papers whiter and the text darker, which is great for receipts and notes; for a proper scan with straightened pages use Scan to PDF. Increase Shadows to brighten faces in backlit photos. Small amounts of sharpness look natural; too much creates halos.

Frequently asked questions

Can it fix very blurry photos?

It sharpens slightly soft photos. Badly blurred or shaky photos can't be fully fixed.

Is the full resolution kept?

Yes, settings are applied to the original size (up to 4000 pixels).

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the page has loaded.

Is my photo uploaded?

No.
