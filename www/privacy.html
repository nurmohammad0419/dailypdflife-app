Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

DailyPdfLife (dailypdflife.com) is built so that your files stay with you. This page explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site.

Your files never leave your device

Every tool on DailyPdfLife — merging, splitting, compressing, editing, converting, OCR, background removal, the CV, invoice and cover-letter makers and the rest — runs entirely inside your web browser. The files you choose and the text you type are processed on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers, we do not store them, and we cannot see them. When you close or reload the page, they are gone.

Scan to PDF can use your device's camera if you allow it. The camera picture is only shown and processed on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Information stored in your browser

A few small preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the site works the way you left it:

  • your chosen language (English or Bangla);
  • on the Invoice & Quotation maker, your business name, details, logo, colour, currency and VAT rate, so you do not have to type them again;
  • on the Zakat calculator, the amounts and prices you typed, so you can come back to them later.

This information stays in your browser. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes technical information needed to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Some tools load free open-source code libraries and AI model files from public content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg and the IMG.LY content network). When your browser downloads these files, those networks receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Your own files are not sent to them.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Children

DailyPdfLife is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
