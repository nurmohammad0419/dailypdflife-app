PDF to Word

Pick a PDF and its text becomes a Word file. For scanned PDFs use the OCR tool.


About PDF to Word

PDF files are great for sharing but hard to change. PDF to Word takes the text from your PDF and puts it into a Word document (.docx) that you can edit in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, WPS Office or LibreOffice. It is the quickest way to reuse the wording of a letter, a CV, a report or a contract without retyping it.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Tap Convert to Word.
  3. Save the .docx file and open it in your word processor.

What to expect

The converter focuses on the text: paragraphs and lines come across so you can edit and reformat them. Complex layouts with many columns, tables, text boxes and images may need some tidying afterwards. For tables of numbers, PDF to Excel gives better results. If your PDF is a scan or a photo of paper, it contains pictures rather than text; use OCR first, which can also save straight to Word.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to Word free?

Yes, with no limits, sign-up or watermark.

Why is my Word file empty?

Your PDF is probably scanned, so the pages are images. Use the OCR tool to read the text instead.

Will fonts and layout stay the same?

The text is kept, but complex layouts may need adjusting in Word.

Is my file uploaded?

No, conversion happens on your device.
