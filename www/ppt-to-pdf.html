About PowerPoint to PDF

A PDF is the safest way to send a presentation: it opens on any phone or computer, looks the same everywhere and can't be changed by accident. This tool reads your .pptx file and draws each slide, with its text, pictures, coloured shapes, tables and background, into a PDF page with the same size as your slides. You don't need PowerPoint installed.

How to convert PowerPoint to PDF

  1. Choose your .pptx file.
  2. Choose the quality and whether to add slide numbers.
  3. Tap Convert to PDF. A preview of every slide appears.
  4. Save the PDF.

What is supported

Text boxes, titles, bullet points, pictures, simple shapes, tables and slide backgrounds are drawn. Charts, SmartArt, videos, animations and some special effects are not, and the tool tells you when it skips something. Slides are saved as high-resolution pictures, so the text in the PDF can't be selected. For perfect results with complex designs, use “Save as PDF” in PowerPoint or Google Slides. Old .ppt files must first be saved as .pptx.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Microsoft Office?

No, the conversion runs in your browser.

Are animations kept?

No, a PDF shows each slide as a still page.

Can I open .ppt files?

Save them as .pptx in PowerPoint or Google Slides first.

Is my presentation uploaded?

No.
